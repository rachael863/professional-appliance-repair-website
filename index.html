Before you request

Questions customers need answered.

The production answers must be approved by the owner and operations team.

How does the service-call or diagnostic fee work?

The diagnostic fee starts at $129 plus applicable sales tax. The exact amount varies by ZIP code because additional travel charges apply in specific areas. The confirmed total should be disclosed before the appointment is booked.

What brands and models do you service?

Professional Appliance Repair works on most appliance brands, including less-common brands such as ILVE, Forno, EuroChef, Verona, THOR, and NXR. Coverage still depends on the appliance, model, configuration, and parts availability, so the team confirms fit during scheduling.

Will I receive an estimate before the repair?

Recommended policy copy: explain that the technician diagnoses the appliance and presents the repair option before authorized work begins. Confirm the exact process before publication.

What warranty coverage applies?

The service-call diagnostic fee is covered for 30 days when the follow-up concerns the same appliance and the same issue. Labor is covered for 30 days for the same issue. Parts coverage is manufacturer-specific and typically ranges from 90 days to one year, depending on the manufacturer. The exact parts terms should be documented on the repair record.

When is the office open?

Regular business hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM–4:30 PM. Appointment availability and arrival windows are confirmed during scheduling.

What professional credentials does the company hold?

Technicians who handle regulated refrigerants hold EPA Section 608 certification, and Professional Appliance Repair is GE Factory Authorized. Manufacturer authorization should be stated only for GE unless additional authorizations are documented.
