Frequently asked questions

Good questions deserve straight answers.

Here are the basics. The office can confirm the details for your appliance, ZIP code, and appointment.

How does the service-call or diagnostic fee work?

The office can confirm the diagnostic visit charge, applicable tax and any travel charge for your address before you book. Ask whether the diagnostic charge is applied to an approved repair.

What brands and models do you service?

The team services many major brands. Coverage depends on the appliance, model, configuration, and parts availability. Share the brand and model so the office can confirm fit.

Will I receive an estimate before the repair?

Ask the office how diagnosis and authorization work for your visit. Review the repair option and price before agreeing to the repair.

What warranty coverage applies?

Ask the office which labor and parts terms apply to your proposed repair, and have the terms documented on the repair record.

When is the office open?

Regular business hours are Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM–4:30 PM. Appointment availability and arrival windows are confirmed during scheduling.

What professional credentials does the company hold?

Ask which technician qualifications or manufacturer authorizations apply to your appliance. The office can confirm them when scheduling.
