Frequently asked questions

Useful answers before the visit.

How much is the diagnostic service call?+

The fee starts at $129 plus sales tax. Additional travel fees apply to certain ZIP codes; the office confirms the total applicable service-call fee before booking.

What ZIP codes do you serve?+

The published list includes 70001, 70002, 70003, 70004, 70005, 70006, 70032, 70043, 70053, 70054, 70055, 70056, 70057, 70058, 70062, 70065, 70072, 70114, 70115, 70116, 70117, 70118, 70119, 70122, 70123, 70124, 70131. Final availability is confirmed during scheduling.

What brands do you service?+

Most appliance brands, including less-common names such as ILVE, Forno, EuroChef, Verona, THOR and NXR. Coverage is confirmed by appliance type and model.

Are you factory authorized?+

Professional Appliance Repair is GE Factory Authorized. That credential should not be read as factory authorization for other brands.

What warranty applies to the visit?+

The diagnostic fee has 30-day coverage for the same appliance and same issue. Labor coverage for the same issue is also 30 days.

How long are parts covered?+

Parts coverage is manufacturer-specific and typically ranges from 90 days to one year.

What should I have ready?+

Your ZIP code, appliance type, brand, model number if available, symptoms and any error code. Mention built-in, stacked or difficult-access installations.

Do technicians handle refrigerants?+

Technicians handling regulated refrigerants hold EPA Section 608 certification.
