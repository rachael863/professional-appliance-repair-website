# Brand concept: thoughtful service, close to home

This design explores a more personal, premium presentation for Professional Appliance Repair. “White glove” describes the desired care and clarity of the experience; it is not a claim that technicians use literal white gloves or offer an unverified service guarantee.

## Positioning

- **Audience:** New Orleans area homeowners who want careful service, clear visit details, and an informed repair choice.
- **Voice:** warm, composed, direct. Speak like a capable local family business, without luxury clichés or urgency tricks.
- **Message:** thoughtful appliance repair for New Orleans homes. Local ownership and a clear first conversation lead the story.
- **Proof:** keep only checkable business facts. The listed fee, ZIP coverage, warranty wording, and any operational promise need owner confirmation before production use.

## Visual language

- Deep river navy and muted cypress green convey steadiness; plaster cream and antique brass add warmth.
- A small `PA` monogram is a **concept mark**, not a representation of an approved company logo.
- Georgia headings give the page an editorial tone without an external font request. Body text stays in the system font for speed and legibility.
- Two generated photographs illustrate the design direction. They are labeled “Concept imagery” in the page, contain no actual employees or customers, and should be replaced with approved photographs if the business has them.
- New Orleans is suggested through residential architecture and light. The page avoids tourist iconography and claims of historic tenure.

## Conversion path

The public prototype offers a direct call, text, and a link to the company's current online request form. It does not collect requests itself. The fee note and service area checker remain qualified, and the page stays `noindex` until any production migration is approved.

## Generated image prompts

Built-in image generation produced both repository assets. The hero prompt requested an editorial scene of a careful, unbranded appliance technician inspecting a refrigerator in an airy New Orleans style home, with warm natural light, subtle shutters, and no text, logo, badge, or unsafe action. The supporting prompt requested an editorial scene of a technician discussing a washer diagnosis with a homeowner in a local home, without identifiable staff or customers, claims, or branding. Both images are illustrative concepts.
